What you play

Luna Tiles is a mahjong tile-matching game. Tap the tiles whose top and side are clear and they travel to a four-slot tray; when two of the same face meet there, the pair clears itself. Fill the tray with nothing matching and the run ends — the real game is digging deep without overflowing it. No timer, no opponent. It plays one-handed, at your own pace, by the light of a bedside lamp.

Highlights

Difficulty that never walls you in

Every level's solvability was measured over thousands of simulated runs. It gets harder without becoming a brick wall.

Hint, undo, shuffle

There when you're stuck. Mistakes are not punished.

Stars and combos

Quick successive matches build a combo; each level is worth up to three stars.

Flower and season tiles

The classic mahjong rule: any flower matches any flower, any season any season.

Plays silent

Music, sound effects and vibration each have their own switch.

No connection needed

On a plane, underground, out of range — it all works.

Privacy, briefly

No account, nothing to hand over. Your progress lives on your device alone. Ads inside the app are served by Google AdMob, which accesses some data for advertising; it is all spelled out in the privacy policy.
